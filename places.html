Places to visit

Montmartre

A hill in the north of Paris with the Sacré-Cœur, small streets and great views over the city.

Canal Saint-Martin

A relaxed area with cafés, where students often meet in the evening.

Luxembourg Gardens

A big park in the centre, good for studying outside or taking a break.

Louvre Museum

One of the largest museums in the world. Check the official website for student prices.
